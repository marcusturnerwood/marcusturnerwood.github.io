Most of the paintings and objects which define Surrealism as a movement are still in copyright across the UK and EU, since the artists who made them mostly lived well into the twentieth century. Every image below is loaded from the collecting museum's website and linked through to that museum's page, so credit and any copyright stay with the museum. Any institutions that do not make a usable image available on their own site, are represented instead by Wikipedia's hosted copy.
