About

I'm Marcus Turner-Wood. This site is a periodically-updated publication of short articles and write-ups on topics I find interesting or important, spanning physics research, mathematics, and philosophy, alongside a record of practical physics projects I've worked on (detector calibration, activation analysis, reactor shielding, and related experimental work).

The articles section covers whatever I've been reading or thinking about at the time; the projects section documents specific pieces of research and lab work, with full reports available where relevant.
